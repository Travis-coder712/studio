Booking tips for the good stuff

National Trust & Landmark Trust properties release ~11–12 months ahead and the best ones (towers, lighthouses, castles) sell out within days — set a reminder to book the moment the 2027 dates open. A big cottage split between the group is often cheaper per head than hotels, and Landmark places sleep 4–12. For Oxford, University Rooms lets you stay inside a college in summer vacation.
