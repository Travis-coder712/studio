Booking the good stuff

Landmark & National Trust properties release ~11–12 months ahead and the iconic ones (towers, lighthouses, castles) vanish in days — diarise the on-sale. A big cottage split between the group usually beats hotels per head, and Landmark places sleep 4–12. For Oxford, University Rooms puts you inside a college over summer vacation. One person should be the "booking captain" and pay the deposit, then split it.
