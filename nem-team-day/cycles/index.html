Topic 1 Market cycles & shocks

The NEM is shaped by the shocks it didn't see coming

Weather and broken machines swing this market harder than any plan. Hazelwood, Callide, the 2022 suspension, Liddell — each redrew the map. You'd never have closed Liddell if you'd known what was coming. Where does today sit against the norm — a calm, well-supplied moment, or one heatwave and one broken boiler from the next one?

◆ The headline — a decade of prices, and what it does to returns

Wholesale prices move in shock-driven cycles. On the chart the swings can look gentle — but a generator's revenue is price × volume off a fixed capex base, so a small $/MWh move is a large move in returns. Reading the cycle — and what it means for each table
  • Table A · Weather — The sharpest peaks (2022, and every summer cap event) are weather-and-fuel shocks: heat, low wind and a global gas/coal spike coinciding. The top of the cycle is a weather story.
  • Table B · Machines — The 2017 step-up follows Hazelwood; the 2024 second peak coincides with an ageing, less-available coal fleet. Closures and outages reset the floor the market bids off.
  • Table C · Counterfactual — 2022 and 2024 are the revenue environments Liddell's 2023 exit landed beside. “What if it had stayed?” is really “what would those two peaks have looked like?”
  • The leverage point — The line's gentle-looking swings hide the returns story: because capex is fixed, moving from a cyclical low to a mid-cycle price can take a project from below its hurdle to comfortably above it — and the fall does the reverse just as fast. That's the panel on the right (edit it to your own numbers), and a fair fifth question for the room.
  • Outturns → the market that funds you — The solid lines are market outturns; the dashed extension is the ASX forward curve (CY27–28 base) — where projects hedge and lenders price risk. It sits low and flat: VIC ~$62–71, NSW ~$85, SA in contango to ~$92. Today's outturns re-rate that curve and the long-term forecasts that underwrite FID — so a cyclical low doesn't just dent this year, it lowers the whole curve the next project is built on.
